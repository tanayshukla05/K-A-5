# THE SHELF — cartridge attribution

Every cartridge pre-installed on cabinet 02 is **homebrew released free by its
own author**. The archive hosts no commercial ROMs — this shelf only carries
games the creators themselves gave away for free. Play them, then visit the
authors and thank them.

| File | Game | Author(s) | Released free at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `super-boss-gaiden.sfc` | Super Boss Gaiden (v1.2b) — action platformer, 2016/2018 | ChronoMoogle & Dieter "D4S" von Laser | https://superbossgaiden.superfamicom.org/ |
| `jet-pilot-rising.sfc` | Jet Pilot Rising (v1.1) — one-button flyer, 2013/2018 | Dieter "d4s" von Laser (music: stargazer) | https://github.com/snesdev0815/SNES-JetPilotRising (author source + ROM release) |
| `uwol-quest-for-money.sfc` | UWOL: Quest for Money (SNES port, 2015) | Original game: The Mojon Twins · SNES port: Alekmaul, music adapted by KungFuFurby from Shiru's Mega Drive version | https://www.portabledev.com/jeux/supernes/uwol-snes/ · https://www.mojontwins.com/juegos_mojonos/uwol-quest-for-money/ |
| `n-warp-daisakusen.sfc` | N-Warp Daisakusen (v1.1) — 8-player arena brawler, 2008 | Matthias "d4s" Nagler (music: maf) | Author-released freeware ("available for download completely free of charge") · mirrored by the libretro buildbot |

Licensing notes:

- Super Boss Gaiden, Jet Pilot Rising and N-Warp Daisakusen are author-released
  freeware — the creators distribute the ROM files themselves, free of charge.
- UWOL: Quest for Money is The Mojon Twins' freeware game; the SNES port is
  distributed by its author Alekmaul on his own site, portabledev.com.

If you are one of these authors and would rather not have your game on this
shelf, contact the site owner and the cartridge will be removed immediately.
