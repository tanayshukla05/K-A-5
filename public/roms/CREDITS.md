# THE SHELF — cartridge attribution

Every cartridge pre-installed on cabinet 02 is **homebrew released free by its
own author**, with three exceptions: `powerfest-94-lost-levels.sfc`,
`yoshis-island-usa.sfc` and `aladdin-usa.sfc` are the site owner's own
cartridges, placed on the shelf for personal play — they are not freeware and
no download of them is offered here.

Licensing notes:

- Super Boss Gaiden, Jet Pilot Rising and N-Warp Daisakusen are author-released
  freeware — the creators distribute the ROM files themselves, free of charge.
- UWOL: Quest for Money is The Mojon Twins' freeware game; the SNES port is
  distributed by its author Alekmaul on his own site, portabledev.com.

If you are one of these authors and would rather not have your game on this
shelf, contact the site owner and the cartridge will be removed immediately.
